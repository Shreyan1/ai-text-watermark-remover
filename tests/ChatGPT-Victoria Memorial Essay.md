# Victoria Memorial, Kolkata

The Victoria Memorial is one of the most famous landmarks of Kolkata and an important symbol of the city's history and culture. Located in the heart of Kolkata, this magnificent marble building was built in memory of Queen Victoria, who was the Empress of India. The construction began in 1906 and was completed in 1921.

The Victoria Memorial is made of beautiful white Makrana marble and combines elements of British and Indian architecture. Its grand domes, tall pillars, sculptures, and spacious gardens give it a majestic appearance. At the top of the central dome is a statue of the Angel of Victory, which adds to the monument's distinctive character.

Today, the Victoria Memorial functions as a museum and houses a large collection of paintings, photographs, manuscripts, coins, weapons, and other historical objects. These exhibits provide visitors with an insight into India's colonial past and the development of Kolkata over the years. The museum also contains several galleries dedicated to different aspects of Indian and British history.

The lush green gardens surrounding the memorial make it a popular destination for both tourists and local residents. It is especially beautiful in the evening when the monument is illuminated.

The Victoria Memorial is more than just a historical building. It represents an important chapter in Kolkata's past and serves as a reminder of India's complex colonial history. Its architecture, museum, and peaceful surroundings make it one of the most cherished landmarks of Kolkata.
